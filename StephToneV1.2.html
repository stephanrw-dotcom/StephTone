How to use StephToneV1.2

StephTone turns any image into a halftone, line, ring or pattern screen. It runs in your browser and nothing is uploaded anywhere.

1 / LOAD AN IMAGE

  • Upload Image opens your file picker. You can also drag a file onto the page or paste with Ctrl/Cmd+V.
  • Photo, Portrait and Colour load the built-in images. Use Colour to see CMYK separations.
  • Invert, Flip and Rotate 90 adjust the image before screening.

2 / START FROM A PRESET

Pick one under Presets and adjust from there. Each preset resets everything first. Type a name and press + Save to keep your own. Reset All starts clean.

3 / USE THE KNOBS

Drag a knob up or down. Hold Shift for fine control, scroll for single steps, double-click to reset. Controls only appear when they apply to the chosen screen.

4 / THE SECTIONS

  • Screen: Unified, or H / M / S split to style highlights, mids and shadows separately. Dots (grid or hex), lines, wavy lines, rings, crosshatch, stochastic or pattern.
  • Tessellation: splits the image into Voronoi, grid or shard cells. Cell Gap, Position Offset and Rotation Variance move each cell. Random Scale scales each cell’s content by a random amount, extreme at full.
  • Tone: contrast, brightness, gamma, Hide Whites (keeps highlights as clean paper), min and max mark, sample smoothing.
  • Texture: position jitter, ink noise, misregistration between plates, seed.
  • Plates: in CMYK mode, view cyan, magenta, yellow or black alone.
  • Pattern: threshold-map screens: noise, serpentines, spirals, arcs.
  • Image Filters: grayscale method, colour filter hue, Image Width (working resolution), blur, sharpen.
  • Colour: Mono, Duo, Tri or CMYK. Choose inks and paper from the swatches or a custom colour. Swap Ink / Paper inverts them.

5 / EXPLORE AND UNDO

  • Undo and Redo sit under the artwork (Ctrl/Cmd+Z and Shift+Z). They cover settings, not which image is loaded.
  • ● Hold to Generate: hold it to keep producing variations (seed, jitter, noise, angle, cell sizes). Let go to stop. The result is one undo step.
  • Press F for Clean View, and ? to open this help.

6 / EXPORT

  • PNG: 1×, 2×, 3×, 4× HR or 6× HR, with a 72, 150 or 300 DPI tag. The status line shows the pixel and print size. Very large exports are capped by browser limits.
  • SVG: true vector with one named layer per ink. Paper Background adds or removes the paper layer. Turn Multiply Plates off if your design app ignores blend modes.

TIPS

  • To stay close to your photo, use a small cell size, low Tessellation and Hide Whites around 6–10.
  • For a print look, use Duo, Tri or CMYK with some misregistration, a warm paper colour and a little ink noise.
  • If the preview lags, use a larger cell size while you adjust and reduce it at the end.
